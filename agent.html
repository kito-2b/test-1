The Daily Brew

A quick cheat sheet for morning coffee ratios.

French Press: 1:15 ratio. Coarse grind, steep for 4 minutes.
Pour Over: 1:17 ratio. Medium-fine grind, steady circular pour.
AeroPress: 1:13 ratio. Fine grind, press slowly for 30 seconds.

Note: Weights are based on grams of coffee to milliliters of water.
